Industrial Demonstration & Academic Scope

This demo is developed for industrial enterprise evaluation, demonstrating that full zero-knowledge encryption can be executed natively in standard browsers without transmitting raw passwords, private keys, or unencrypted data over network wires. Every button, cipher option, and audit trail in this web application is fully operational and powered by the W3C Web Cryptography API.
